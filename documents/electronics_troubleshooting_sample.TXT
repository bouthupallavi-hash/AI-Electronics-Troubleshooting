Electronics Troubleshooting Guide
LED Does Not Turn On
Possible causes: incorrect LED polarity, missing or incorrect resistor, disconnected power supply, or incorrect
wiring. Troubleshooting: check the power supply, verify LED polarity, inspect the series resistor, and check
the wiring.
Resistor Becomes Hot
Possible causes: excessive current, incorrect resistor value, insufficient power rating, or a short circuit.
Troubleshooting: disconnect power when appropriate, verify the resistor value, check the circuit connections,
and compare the expected circuit operation with the design.
Circuit Does Not Turn On
Possible causes: disconnected power, discharged battery, open fuse, loose connection, or a short circuit.
Troubleshooting: check the power source, switch, wiring, connectors, and fuse.
Diode Circuit Problem
Possible causes: reversed polarity, incorrect diode type, damaged diode, or incorrect wiring. Troubleshooting:
disconnect power, verify diode orientation and type, inspect connections, and use an appropriate multimeter
test if available.
Transistor Circuit Problem
Possible causes: incorrect pin connections, incorrect biasing, wrong transistor type, or damaged transistor.
Troubleshooting: verify the part number and pin configuration using the datasheet, inspect surrounding
components, and check the supply.
Safety
Disconnect power before modifying a circuit when appropriate. Do not work on exposed mains-voltage or
high-voltage circuits without proper training and supervision. If dangerous voltage, smoke, burning
components, or hazardous conditions are involved, stop troubleshooting and contact a qualified technician
